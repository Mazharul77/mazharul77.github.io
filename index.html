About Me

I'm a Senior Odoo Developer at Betopia Group in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I lead development of custom Odoo solutions for manufacturing, distribution, retail and HR, from data modelling and access control to the OWL front end and reporting.

Before Betopia, I worked as a Software Engineer at Mir Info Systems and Daffodil Computers PLC, building Odoo ERP solutions, and earlier at Codexpert and Goinnovior Technology. What I Do

The roles I work in day to day, for companies in Bangladesh and clients worldwide.

Odoo Developer & Senior Software Engineer

Senior software engineer specialising in Odoo ERP (versions 13 to 19). I design and build custom Odoo modules in Python, PostgreSQL, OWL and QWeb, and migrate modules between Odoo versions.

Odoo Functional Consultant

I translate business requirements into working ERP flows: sales, purchase, inventory, manufacturing (MRP), POS, HR, payroll and accounting configuration, approval workflows, access rights and user training.

AI Integration Engineer

I connect Odoo with AI: AI engine and MCP (Model Context Protocol) server modules that let LLM-based assistants work with ERP data, plus computer-vision projects with OpenCV and MediaPipe.

Full-Stack Web & App Developer

Web developer experienced in Django, React, Node.js, PHP/Laravel and JavaScript. I build REST APIs that power mobile apps for field sales and delivery approval.

Module Testing & QA

Deployment & DevOps for Odoo

Odoo Accounting Expert

Machine Learning & Data Science

Data Engineering & Reporting
